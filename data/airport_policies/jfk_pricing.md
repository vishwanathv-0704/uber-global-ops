# JFK Airport Pricing Policy

## Policy ID
JFK-PRICING-001

## Airport
JFK – John F. Kennedy International Airport

## Purpose
Define pricing and surge controls for JFK airport operations.

## Surge Multiplier Rules

| Surge Range | Status | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0x – 1.2x | Permitted | No approval required |
| >1.2x – 1.6x | Permitted | Operations Manager approval required |
| >1.6x | Not permitted | Cannot be executed |

## Maximum Surge

The maximum permitted surge multiplier at JFK is **1.6x**.

The system must never execute a surge multiplier above 1.6x.

## Conditions for Increasing Surge

A surge increase may be considered when:
- Completion rate falls below 83%.
- Request volume significantly exceeds available supply.
- Queue size exceeds 160 drivers.
- Average ETA exceeds 17 minutes.

Operational telemetry must be reviewed before recommending a surge increase.

## Approval Requirements

Any surge increase above 1.2x requires Operations Manager approval.

An increase to 1.6x is classified as a high-impact operational action and requires explicit human approval.

## Prohibited Actions

Operations must not:

- Set surge above 1.6x.
- Execute an unapproved increase above 1.2x.
- Bypass human approval.
- Increase surge without reviewing operational telemetry.

## Audit Requirements

Every surge override must record:

- Airport
- Previous multiplier
- New multiplier
- Reason
- Approver
- Timestamp

## Policy Summary

JFK permits surge up to **1.6x**.
Surge above **1.2x requires Operations Manager approval**.
Surge above **1.6x is prohibited**.
