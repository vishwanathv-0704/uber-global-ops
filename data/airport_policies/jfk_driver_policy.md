# JFK Airport Driver Policy

## Policy ID
JFK-DRIVER-001

## Airport
JFK – John F. Kennedy International Airport

## Purpose
Define driver queue and pickup requirements for JFK airport.

## Queue Rules

Drivers participating in airport operations must join the designated airport queue.

Drivers should maintain their position while waiting for eligible airport requests.

Intentional queue bypassing is prohibited.

## Queue Abandonment

Drivers may leave the queue voluntarily.

Leaving the queue removes the driver from the current request cycle.

Repeated abandonment and re-entry may be flagged for operational review.

## Pickup Rules

Drivers must use designated airport pickup areas.

Drivers must follow airport traffic and pickup instructions.

Passenger pickup from unauthorized locations is prohibited.

## Driver Cancellation
Drivers should avoid cancelling accepted airport trips without a legitimate operational reason.

A driver cancellation rate above 16% should trigger operational investigation.

## Driver Incentives

Operations may recommend incentives when supply is insufficient.

The recommendation should consider:

- Required additional drivers
- Demand level
- Severity
- Estimated total incentive cost

## Compliance

Drivers must follow airport queue and pickup procedures.

Repeated violations may be escalated to operations for review.
