# SFO Airport Driver Policy

## Policy ID
SFO-DRIVER-001

## Airport
SFO – San Francisco International Airport

## Purpose
Define driver queue and airport operating requirements at SFO.

## Queue Rules

Drivers entering the airport driver pool must join the designated virtual airport queue.

Drivers are expected to maintain their position in the queue.

Drivers must not intentionally bypass the queue to obtain airport requests.

## Queue Abandonment

A driver may leave the airport queue voluntarily.

Leaving the queue removes the driver from consideration for the current airport request cycle.

Repeated intentional queue abandonment and re-entry may be flagged for operational review.

## Pickup Rules

Drivers receiving an airport request must proceed to the designated pickup area.

Drivers must follow airport traffic and pickup instructions.

Drivers must not collect passengers from unauthorized locations.

## Driver Cancellation

Drivers should avoid cancelling accepted airport trips unless there is a legitimate operational reason.

A high driver cancellation rate may trigger an airport operations investigation.

## Incentives

Driver incentives may be considered when available driver supply is insufficient to meet demand.

Any incentive recommendation should consider:

- Supply shortage
- Demand level
- Severity of operational disruption
- Estimated cost

## Compliance

Drivers must comply with applicable airport queue and pickup procedures.

Repeated violations may be escalated for operational review.

## Policy Summary

Drivers must use the airport queue system and follow designated pickup procedures.

Operational teams should investigate sustained increases in driver cancellation rates.
