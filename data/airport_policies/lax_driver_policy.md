# LAX Airport Driver Policy

## Policy ID
LAX-DRIVER-001

## Airport
LAX – Los Angeles International Airport

## Purpose
Define driver queue, pickup, and cancellation requirements at LAX.

## Queue Rules

Drivers participating in airport pickup operations must use the designated virtual queue.

Drivers should maintain their queue position while waiting for airport requests.

Intentional queue bypassing is prohibited.

## Queue Abandonment

Drivers may leave the airport queue.

Leaving the queue removes the driver from the current airport request cycle.

Repeated queue abandonment and re-entry may be reviewed by operations.

## Pickup Rules

Drivers must collect passengers from designated pickup zones.

Drivers must follow airport traffic instructions.

Drivers must not accept airport passengers from unauthorized locations.

## Driver Cancellation

Drivers should minimize cancellations after accepting airport trips.

Sustained cancellation rates above 14% should trigger operational investigation.

## Incentives

Operations may recommend incentives when driver supply is insufficient.

Recommended incentives should consider:

- Number of additional drivers required
- Severity of disruption
- Current demand
- Estimated financial impact

## Compliance

Drivers must follow airport queue and pickup procedures.

Repeated violations may be escalated for review.
